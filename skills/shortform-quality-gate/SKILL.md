# shortform-quality-gate

Score evidence-based quality out of 100.

| Dimension | Max |
|---|---:|
| Market / format demand | 10 |
| Character/IP fit | 10 |
| First-frame stop power | 15 |
| Promise clarity | 10 |
| Visible progression | 10 |
| Continuity / production consistency | 10 |
| Payoff or reversal | 15 |
| Repeatable series potential | 10 |
| Platform package fit | 5 |
| Production feasibility | 5 |
| TOTAL | 100 |

## Gate
- 85-100 GREEN — MAKE
- 72-84 YELLOW — REVISE weakest high-weight item, then rescore
- 0-71 RED — KILL or redesign premise

## Hard fails
A concept cannot be GREEN if any of these is unresolved:
- character identity drifts between scenes
- ending payoff is missing
- first frame does not communicate the event/problem
- state continuity is contradictory
- concept is only a surface clone of another creator
