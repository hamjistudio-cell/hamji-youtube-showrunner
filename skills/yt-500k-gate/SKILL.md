# yt-500k-gate

Score each dimension using evidence.

| Dimension | Max |
|---|---:|
| Market Demand | 15 |
| Outlier Evidence | 15 |
| Promise Clarity | 10 |
| Title + Thumbnail Synergy | 15 |
| Hook Strength | 10 |
| Retention Architecture | 10 |
| Payoff Strength | 10 |
| Channel Fit | 5 |
| Series Potential | 5 |
| Production Feasibility | 5 |
| TOTAL | 100 |

## Gate
80-100 GREEN
70-79 YELLOW
0-69 RED

## Rules
- Missing evidence lowers confidence.
- Do not award high Market or Outlier scores without evidence.
- Great packaging cannot rescue a weak payoff.
- Great topic cannot rescue a misleading package.
- If RED, repair the single weakest high-weight dimension first.
- Re-score after revision.
