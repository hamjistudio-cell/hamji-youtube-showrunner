# yt-package

Title and thumbnail are ONE promise.

## Rules
- Do not repeat the same words in title and thumbnail unless necessary.
- Thumbnail text: ideally 0-3 words.
- The pair must communicate one clear outcome, conflict, transformation, discovery, or curiosity gap.
- Prefer concrete nouns, numbers, names, dates, visible outcomes, and contrasts over vague adjectives.
- Avoid claims the video cannot prove.
- Mobile readability wins.

## Create 10 packages
For each:
TITLE
THUMB TEXT
THUMB VISUAL
PROMISE
CURIOSITY GAP
RISK

Then choose top 3 and explain:
- why someone would click
- what expectation must be paid off
- what could make it feel clickbait

Never package a weak idea into fake urgency.
