# yt-market

Goal: find demand before inventing content.

## Look for
- repeated audience questions
- pains with money/time/status/identity
- new tools, rules, releases, controversies, seasonal moments
- evergreen topics receiving renewed attention
- successful videos with weak coverage in the user's language/format
- topics with multiple recent outliers across different channels

## Output
1. Top 5 demand clusters
2. Evidence for each
3. Saturation level: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH
4. Fresh angle available?
5. Best opportunity this week
6. What NOT to chase

Never treat one viral video as a market.
